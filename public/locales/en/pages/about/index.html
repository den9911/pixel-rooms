A minute about the project

Minute Club is a collection of little games and creative activities for a short break.

What we save

Shared votes, captions, polls, rooms, and daily results are saved on the server. A random identifier in the minute_session cookie connects your actions. No name, email, or registration is required. IP addresses are temporarily counted in server memory to limit excessive requests.

Drawing, document, and room drafts and your personal results stay in this browser’s localStorage. Drawings and documents are not sent to the server. There are no remote fonts, analytics, advertising scripts, or third-party CDNs.

What others can see

Published captions, approved photos, and saved rooms are public. Polls are available to anyone with the link. Remove other people’s personal information before uploading and only publish material you have permission to use.

Photos and reports

Images are compressed on your device to 500 KB. Originals and metadata are not uploaded. A moderator reviews new images. Posts have a report button; a moderator can remove a post and its image.

Cookies and drafts

Clearing cookies removes access to editing your room. Public posts remain on the server. To request removal, use the report button and explain why. Clear site data in your browser to delete local drafts.

Limitations

One vote means one vote per browser session. These are entertainment polls, not identity verification. Daily challenges change on Moscow time. Excuse Bureau documents are for fun and have no legal validity.
